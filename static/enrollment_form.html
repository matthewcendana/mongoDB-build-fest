Medicare Annual Wellness Visit

Enrollment & Consent Record

Purpose. This visit is a yearly wellness check covered by Medicare, used to build or update your personalized prevention plan — not to treat a new illness today.

Information collected. Your health history, current medications, and answers about daily activities and mood, so your care team can recommend screenings and follow-up.

How it may be shared. With your primary care clinician and members of your care team for this visit only. This information is not sold.
